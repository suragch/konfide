# Privacy Policy for Konfide

**Last updated:** October 12, 2026

## Overview

Konfide ("we," "our," or "the app"), developed by Suragch, is a conversation starter app designed to prompt meaningful, face-to-face conversations. We respect your privacy and believe that your personal thoughts, notes, and conversations belong entirely to you.

Konfide is built with an **offline-first, privacy-by-design architecture**. The app does not require user accounts, does not collect personal data, does not transmit data over the internet, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

Konfide does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information to external servers or third parties. Specifically:

- **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, or unique device identifiers (such as IDFA, GAID, or Android ID).
- **No Account or Login Required:** You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to use any feature of Konfide.
- **No Network Transmission or Remote Servers:** Konfide does not connect to external servers, cloud databases, or backend APIs. All features, question packs, and typography fonts are bundled locally within the app.
- **No Financial Information:** Konfide does not process payments, collect payment details, or store billing information.
- **No Location Data:** Konfide does not access, request, or track your GPS or network location.
- **No Biometric Data:** Konfide does not collect or process biometric identifiers.

---

## 2. On-Device Data Storage

All data created or modified while using Konfide remains exclusively on your local device. This data is stored securely in the app's private sandbox storage using a local SQLite database and device preferences (`SharedPreferences` on Android, `UserDefaults` on iOS / macOS).

This locally stored data includes:

- **Companion Profiles:** You may optionally create companion profiles (such as a friend's, partner's, or family member's name/nickname and a chosen accent color) to track conversation progress with specific people. This information never leaves your device.
- **Custom Decks & Questions:** Any custom question decks or questions you write or edit are saved exclusively in your local database.
- **Favorites & Notes:** Questions you bookmark as favorites and any personal notes or reflections you write on questions are saved locally on your device.
- **Deck Progress:** The app keeps track of viewed questions and current card indexes per companion to prevent repetitive cards and maintain your place in a deck.
- **User Preferences:** Your selected visual theme palette and active companion preference are stored locally.

---

## 3. User-Initiated Sharing and File Exchange

Konfide provides optional features that allow you to share content or backup decks. These actions are **always initiated manually by you**:

- **Card Sharing:** You may choose to share individual question cards as formatted text or as rendered card images. This feature utilizes your device's native operating system share sheet. Konfide does not monitor, collect, or store shared text, generated images, or recipient information.
- **Deck Export & Import:** You may export custom question decks as JSON files or import question decks via the system file picker. Temporary files created during the export process are stored in your device's temporary cache directory and managed by your operating system. Konfide only reads files that you explicitly choose to import.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services, Analytics, and Advertising

- **No Advertising:** Konfide contains no advertisements, advertising networks, or advertising SDKs.
- **No Analytics or Tracking:** We do not use third-party analytics services or crash-reporting services that gather personal data or usage patterns.
- **No Tracking Across Apps:** Konfide does not track your activity across other apps, services, or websites, nor does it share data with data brokers.
- **Offline Typography:** Typography fonts are bundled locally as app assets. The app explicitly disables runtime font downloads, preventing external network requests.

---

## 5. Device Permissions

Konfide requests minimal system permissions strictly required for core functionality:

- **Network / Internet Access:** Konfide does not request or use internet permissions on Android (`android.permission.INTERNET` is not declared) and makes no network requests on iOS.
- **Haptic Feedback:** Used to provide subtle tactile vibrations when swiping cards and interacting with controls.
- **Storage / File Access:** Handled through standard system dialogs (native file picker and share sheets) when you explicitly choose to import or export deck files. No broad storage permissions are requested.
- **No Sensitive Permissions:** Konfide never requests access to your camera, microphone, contacts list, calendar, photo library, or location.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data resides solely on your physical device, you retain full and complete control over your data:

- **In-App Deletion:** You can delete custom decks, custom questions, companion profiles, and personal question notes at any time directly within the app interface.
- **Complete Deletion:** You can permanently erase all locally stored data, notes, custom decks, companions, and preferences at any time by:
  - Clearing the app's storage / data through your device's system settings, or
  - Uninstalling the Konfide app from your device.

Once uninstalled or cleared, all local data is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

Konfide is suitable for general audiences and does not target children specifically. We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable jurisdictions). Because Konfide does not collect any personal data from any user, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and platform requirements for families.

---

## 8. Security

We take the security of your data seriously. Because Konfide stores all data locally on your device, your information is protected by the built-in hardware and operating system security features of Android, iOS, and macOS, including application sandboxing and device passcode/biometric protection.
