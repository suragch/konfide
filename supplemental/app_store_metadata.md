## Google Play Store

### Short description

Conversation starters to deepen the relationships in your life

### Full description

This "social" app won't have your staring at your phone all day. It doesn't have a chat feature and there's no way to post your status. It's just a single-feature app that's intended to help you have quality conversations face-to-face with the person right next to you.

The app contains packs of conversation starter questions for everyone from casual acquaintances to deep friends. It's great for getting to know people better, even those who you might have known for years.
